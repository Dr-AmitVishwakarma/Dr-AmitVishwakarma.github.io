About

Research-driven AI with real-world application

I am an Applied AI Scientist, researcher and educator working on machine learning, Generative AI, intelligent decision support systems, blockchain and sustainable digital transformation.

My work focuses on translating interdisciplinary research into practical systems for supply chains, carbon management, energy trading, public procurement and digital governance. I have worked across IIT Kanpur, Tsinghua University, Samsung R&D Bangalore, the Ministry of Finance, Khalifa University and IIM Raipur.
